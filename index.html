Assignment 1.4 · AI Lab

CyberSafe AI: Your Friendly Online Safety Assistant

A beginner-friendly cybersecurity education chatbot prototype developed in Mizou.

CyberSafe AI is designed to help everyday users, students, and employees recognize phishing and social-engineering risks and take safer actions. The project included LLM experimentation, Custom GPT exploration, STORM AI research, design thinking, chatbot development, and three initial prototype tests.

Testing and learning

  • Tested a suspicious bank SMS, a suspicious bank login URL, and password reuse.
  • Observed generally useful beginner-level guidance and identified a repeated incorrect personalization issue (“Raj”).
  • Documented limitations: no live URL validation or malware scanning, and AI responses require independent verification.
